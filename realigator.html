Realigator: Property & Lease OS

B2B Enterprise SaaS Real Estate Management Expo & React Native Web Supabase Cloud Sync

A comprehensive, modern cross-platform property and rental management platform designed for landlords, property management firms, and real estate operators. Features expandable multi-unit building hierarchies, tenant KYC directories, itemized rent billing, instant PDF receipt generation, and real-time cashflow telemetry.

VIEW ON GitHub Deploy for Business

Core Operational Architecture & Capabilities

Multi-Unit Apartment Hierarchy

Model complex multi-floor apartment complexes and standalone commercial properties. Expandable floor and unit structures with high-resolution photo galleries, floor specs, and target rent rates.

Batch Floor & Flat Generator

Onboard residential properties in seconds. Generate multi-floor unit matrices (e.g. Floors 1–10 × Units A, B, C) in a single click with default rents and service charge templates.

Tenants, KYC & Leases

Track active and historic leases, security deposit escrow accounts, and tenant KYC verification records (NID/Passport numbers, emergency contacts, occupations) in a unified CRM view.

Automated Ledger & PDF Receipts

Itemized rent billing calculating Base Rent, Service Charges, and Utilities with Paid, Partial, and Overdue states. Instant 1-click printable PDF rent receipts with landlord branding powered by expo-print.

Live Cashflow & P&L Reporting

Telemetry dashboard tracking Net Cashflow (MTD), Rent Collection Rate, and Portfolio Occupancy. Building-by-building Profit & Loss (P&L) calculations, expense tracking, and 1-click CSV report exports.

Offline-First with Supabase Cloud Sync

Zero-latency local storage persistence ensures property managers can audit flats and log offline collections without an internet connection, synchronizing seamlessly to Supabase PostgreSQL when online.
